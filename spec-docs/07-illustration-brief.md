# Hearth Swap — Illustration Brief
## DS-12: Logo & Isis Mascot

---

### The project

Hearth Swap is a neighborhood bartering app — no money, just neighbors trading what they have for what they need. The vibe is warm, abundant, and grounded. Think a farmers market run by people who actually trust each other.

The brand is female-forward in feeling — warm, abundant, capable — without being exclusive. Men use it too and should feel welcome. The energy is less "girlboss" and more "wise woman who grows her own food and always has what you need."

---

### What we need

Two deliverables:

1. **A wordmark logo** — the text "Hearth Swap" styled to feel like it belongs with the Isis mascot. Can be standalone or paired with the mascot as a lockup.
2. **The Isis mascot** — an illustrated character based on Isis, Egyptian goddess of magic, healing, and abundance. She is the brand's spirit — not a literal religious icon, but a symbolic anchor for the feeling of the app.

---

### Isis — who she is

Isis is an Egyptian goddess (not Greek — this is a common mix-up). She is associated with:
- **Magic and transformation** — she finds a way
- **Healing and protection** — she takes care of her community
- **Motherhood and abundance** — she provides
- **Wings** — her most iconic visual symbol; she is often depicted with wide, outstretched wings

For Hearth Swap, she represents the abundance that exists when neighbors trust each other. She is not a warrior. She is a giver, a connector, a protector of home.

---

### Visual direction

**Feel:** Warm, elegant, slightly modern. Not overly ornate or "ancient Egypt" — we don't want hieroglyphics and gold pharaoh vibes. We want something that feels rooted in that mythology but lives comfortably in a contemporary app.

**Style reference:** Think editorial illustration — clean lines, intentional use of negative space, a little bit of magic in the details. Closer to a modern book cover illustration than a cartoon mascot.

**She should feel:** Grounded. Wise. Warm. Slightly otherworldly but not cold or intimidating.

---

### Palette

Use the brand colors:

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dusty mauve | `#8B4A6B` | Primary — her clothing, key elements |
| Warm gold | `#C4922A` | Accents — jewelry, wing tips, glow |
| Sage green | `#6B8F71` | Secondary — nature elements if any |
| Warm cream | `#FAF6F0` | Background, skin tones, light |

The illustration should work on a white/cream background and also as a dark silhouette or single-color version for favicon/icon use.

---

### Specific usage contexts

| Context | Format needed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App favicon | 32×32px | Must read as a simple shape — likely just her head or a wing |
| Nav wordmark | ~40px tall | Lockup of icon + "Hearth Swap" text |
| Hero section | ~300×400px | The main illustration, shown on the logged-out home page |
| Profile page banner | SVG or PNG | Optional: a subtle wing motif as a background texture |

---

### What to avoid

- Heavy gold outlines and Egyptian tomb aesthetics — too literal
- Overly cute or cartoon-y — she should feel wise, not kawaii
- Anything that reads as exclusively for women — the brand is female-spirited but not gendered
- Complicated details that won't survive at 32px — the core mark needs to be simple enough for a favicon

---

### Deliverables

- [ ] Mascot illustration — SVG preferred, PNG acceptable (transparent background)
- [ ] Logo wordmark — SVG
- [ ] Lockup (mascot + wordmark together) — SVG
- [ ] Favicon-ready version — 32×32 and 64×64 PNG
- [ ] Single-color/silhouette version for dark backgrounds

---

### Typography in use (for wordmark reference)

The app uses **Cormorant Garamond** (serif, elegant, slightly editorial) for headings and the wordmark. The wordmark should either use this typeface or something that pairs naturally with it. Do not use a sans-serif for the wordmark.
